During my use of the service, I noticed the following positive aspects that really helped me in sharing my materials and content through the web.

- An easy and intuitive interface to use

- Link management capable of implementing and modifying up to the most advanced options, such as:
 - UTM Builder
 - Traffic Routing
 - Retargeting scripts
 - OpenGraph metadata

![Rebrandly link management](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/2nl51wqdr753fkba7akh.png)

- A clear view of analytics related to clicks, country/device, and platform of origin

- Great way for mass import of my links

- The possibility to create a page called 'Link Gallery' that displays our links in one place. 
Definitely useful for sharing, for example, on Instagram along with our various social channels or services that we offer.

![Rebrandly Link Gallery](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/vpx247ihiykwq28uubqe.png)
